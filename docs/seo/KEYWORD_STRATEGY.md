## Method and evidence

Searched the web for website/e-commerce/mobile development in Sri Lanka; AI automation and technical SEO in Colombo; custom software development; Flutter app services; and AI chatbot/workflow services. ## Editorial rules and prioritization

1. Website development is the first commercial priority because the repository supplies authentic website examples. Commerce is second, with a concept storefront and explicit payment/operations caveats. AI, apps and platforms remain valid service offers, but their portfolio evidence is weaker.
2. Each service owns a distinct intent; website, commerce, apps and custom software should not compete through repeated generic copy. The homepage owns agency/brand intent, and the hub supports selection.
3. Avoid separate Colombo/city variants and chatbot/workflow doorway pages. Supporting phrases belong in the relevant section when they match useful content. Do not add a meta-keywords field.
4. Do not claim pricing, delivery schedules, client outcomes, language support, provider partnerships or capabilities solely because competitors mention them.
5. Assess query/page overlap in Search Console after launch. Consolidate overlapping pages or improve positioning only with actual query and conversion evidence.
