# ASL: 90-day organic-growth roadmap

Starts after the owner approves and deploys this local implementation. Recommendations are based on the qualitative keyword research and available repository evidence; they are not traffic forecasts.

## Days 1–30: Establish trustworthy foundations

| Priority | Work | Evidence required | Outcome to monitor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Confirm www canonical host, production/preview indexing and Formspree delivery; complete owner checklist | Deployment settings and a controlled owner test enquiry | Correct canonical/indexability; accepted brief delivered to actual inbox |
| P0 | Set up Search Console and Bing; submit sitemap and inspect all nine pages | Owner access and deployed output | Submitted/selected canonical, discovery, indexing exclusions |
| P1 | Confirm business display name, country, official contact and social profiles | Actual owner-supplied business facts | Consistent visible identity and verified `sameAs` only |
| P1 | Classify each portfolio item and obtain permission for any client case study | Ownership, scope, production status, permission and screenshots | Truthful labels; portfolio-to-enquiry journey |
| P1 | Review six service scopes against the services ASL can actually deliver | Owner sign-off; provider/platform details | Relevant enquiries by service; clear scope |
| P1 | Establish field performance and lead baseline with an approved measurement method | Consent decision, real users and sufficient sample | Search clicks, landing pages, accepted briefs, conversion rate; no speculative field pass |

## Days 31–60: Publish decision-helping content

Prepare drafts; publish only after technical and business review. Two substantial guides are more useful than many keyword variants.

| Suggested piece | Visitor question | Evidence / publishing gate | Internal links |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A website brief for Sri Lankan cafés and salons | What should I prepare before commissioning a site? | Real ASL intake questions; menu/service/booking scope verified; no guaranteed schedules | Website development and contact |
| Planning an online store: catalogue, delivery and payment questions | What does my business need beyond a storefront? | Actual integration capability and provider documentation; don't imply concept checkout processes payments | E-commerce and custom software |
| Website or mobile app: choosing a practical first release | Do my customers need an app? | Supported Flutter/platform scope and realistic deployment constraints | Mobile apps and website development |
| A studio showcase walkthrough | How was this experience designed and built? | Genuine source, screenshots, technology, authorship, permission; clearly label concept status | Projects and relevant service |

Choose the first two using actual enquiries and Search Console data, not invented demand. Define one reader task, one primary intent and one useful CTA per article. Do not publish separate location pages with copied content.

## Days 61–90: Build original evidence and refine

- Document a focused automation pilot with anonymized inputs, human review, error cases, provider costs and observed results. Publish only with permission; do not present the homepage scripted study as live AI work.
- Write a reproducible technical-performance case study if ASL has comparable before/after measurements. Specify devices, throttling, cache, number of runs and field-data availability. Avoid declaring a field CWV pass from lab tools.
- Add a client case study only when the relationship, problem, actual solution, screenshots and results can be verified. Otherwise retain an explicitly labelled studio walkthrough.
- Review query-to-page fit and enquiry quality. Improve under-explained sections and internal links. Consolidate duplicated intent before creating another route.
- Share useful published work through verified official profiles; obtain permission before publishing client details. Seek relevant genuine citations and relationships, not bought links.

## Review rhythm

Weekly during initial launch: indexing errors, failed form deliveries, broken links and material regressions. Monthly: compare query groups, landing pages, qualified enquiries and source attribution where available. Use comparable periods and note small samples. Revisit the roadmap at day 90 using actual data.

Publication backlog needs owner approval for: prices, timelines, case-study claims, verified client names, Colombo office/service-area claims, social URLs, app screenshots, payment provider support and production AI examples. No article/blog routes were added merely to fill this roadmap.
